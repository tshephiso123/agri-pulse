# Crop checks and immediate guidance

The user expanded crop selection to maize, tomato, beans and cabbage during phase 4. Maize keeps the bundled FAW observation checklist and its cautious possible/uncertain/no-reported-sign outcomes. The result now includes the complete relevant management list immediately, with individual guided steps still available.

Tomato, beans and cabbage have a general early-symptom triage flow: chewing/insects, unusual leaf spots, and wilting/poor growth. These observations do not identify a specific pest or disease. Results show inspection, recordkeeping, local adviser confirmation and treatment-label guidance. There are no invented crop-specific diagnoses, treatment thresholds or pesticide doses.

The general guidance is adapted from the inspection, identification and recordkeeping principles in [UF/IFAS, Chapter 4: Integrated Pest Management](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/CV298). The source describes Florida production; the app uses general inspection principles and refers farmers to their own local adviser rather than importing regional treatment thresholds. Local agronomic validation is still required. Existing maize guidance retains its FAO/CIMMYT references.

New strings are English only and listed in translations-needed.md. They fall back to bundled English in Sepedi/Tshivenda/Xitsonga until the localization lead provides reviewed translations.

Farm setup asks language first, then optional farm name/community, crops grown and optional hectares. Setup preferences are stored locally, outside the encrypted logbook, and never sent through sync. Private field notes remain in the protected logbook. If preferences cannot be stored, farmers can continue for the current session. Crops are available even before an account or logbook passphrase is created. The calculator retains its existing maize/tomato sample rates; no rates are invented for beans or cabbage.
